# AI Tally – download

AI Tally prepares vouchers from bank statements, sales/purchase registers and journals, checks them, and posts them
to TallyPrime **only after you approve them**. It runs on your own PC – your data stays with you.

## Download

**[Download the latest AI Tally installer](https://github.com/sggunjal-creator/sggunjal-creator-AI-Tally-Download/releases/latest)** – open the page and click
`AI-Tally-Setup-<version>.exe` under **Assets**.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- TallyPrime on the same PC
- An administrator password for this PC (only to install)

## Install

1. Run `AI-Tally-Setup-<version>.exe`.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the installer is not code-signed yet.
   Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Follow the setup screens. "Getting started" opens at the end – follow it to connect TallyPrime.

The program goes to `C:\Program Files\AI Tally`. Your data (database, logs, daily backups) goes to
`C:\ProgramData\AI Tally`. Updates and uninstalling never delete your data.

## Trial

Without a licence file, AI Tally runs as a **15-day trial** (up to 3 companies, 5 users). After that it becomes
read-only until a licence file is uploaded. No data is ever deleted.

## Test safely

- Every company starts **READ_ONLY**. Test on a **demo company in TEST mode** first – not on a client company.
- Nothing reaches Tally until a checker approves and posts it.

## Update to a new version

Download the new installer from the same link and run it over the existing installation. Your companies, history,
rules and audit log are kept, and the database is backed up automatically first.

## Problems?

In AI Tally open **Help & support → Download support file** and send the .zip to the person who gave you this link.
By default it contains no company, party or ledger names and no amounts.
